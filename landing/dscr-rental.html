DSCR Loan FAQs

What is DSCR?

DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio. It compares rental income to loan payments.

Do I need personal income docs?

No. DSCR loans are based on property cash flow, not personal income.

Can I use this for short-term rentals?

Yes. DSCR loans can be used for Airbnb, VRBO, and other short-term rental properties.
